Step 1 — Set total leave balance per student

Enter the total number of Earned Leaves (EL) and Casual Leaves (CL) allotted to each student for the term. Once set, click the EL / CL buttons on each student row to use up their leave. The balance decreases automatically.

Step 2 — Use leaves (click EL or CL to deduct from balance)

Each click on EL or CL deducts 1 day from that student's balance. These are not counted as absences.
