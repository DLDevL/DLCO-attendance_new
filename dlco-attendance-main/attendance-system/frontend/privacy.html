Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Attendance Register (“the app”) is a student project for recording and viewing class attendance. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see your password in plain text).
  • Google sign-in: if you choose “Continue with Google”, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Profile details you enter: first and last name, phone number and role (student or teacher).
  • Attendance records: classes, roll numbers, attendance marks and leave balances entered by teachers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure, including sending a confirmation email when you sign up.
  • To show teachers and students the attendance information relevant to them.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or share your data with anyone except the services needed to run the app (Supabase for accounts and data storage, Vercel for hosting, and Google if you use Google sign-in).

Storage and security

Data is stored in a Supabase database protected by access rules. Students can only see their own attendance. Some preferences (such as the light/dark theme) are saved only in your browser.

Your choices

You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time by emailing us. If you used Google sign-in, you can also remove the app’s access from your Google Account’s security settings.
